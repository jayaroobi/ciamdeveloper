## Interview prep — AM / SAML questions

Prepare stories from **your lab**, not employer internals:

1. Walk through SAML AuthnRequest → Response flow
2. What is a Circle of Trust?
3. Difference between hosted vs remote IdP/SP
4. How does PingDS idrepo differ from ds-cts?
5. OIDC vs SAML — when would you choose each?
6. What is a non-human identity and how do you rotate credentials?
